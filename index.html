What Front Caller does

A capable front desk for every incoming call.

Front Caller follows the information and call flow you approve, giving callers a consistent answer while keeping your team in control.

Answers every call

Provides a professional response during busy periods, after hours, and whenever your staff cannot pick up.

Books appointments

Checks the booking process you configure, gathers the right details, and schedules eligible appointments.

Handles common questions

Responds using your approved services, business hours, policies, pricing information, and frequently asked questions.

Captures qualified leads

Collects names, contact details, needs, and relevant context so your team can follow up with a useful record.

Transfers priority callers

Recognizes the escalation conditions you define and routes important calls to the appropriate person.

Keeps your team informed

Organizes call outcomes, lead details, and bookings so staff can quickly understand what happened and act next.

How it works

From first ring to the right outcome.
